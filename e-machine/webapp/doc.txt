05. GRID & RESPONSIVENESS
--------------------------------------------------
Approach: Mobile First

Mobile:
- 2 product columns.
- 3 category columns.
- 16–20px horizontal padding.
- Full-width product details.
- Sticky header.
- Horizontal scroll for category filters.

Tablet:
- 3 product columns.
- Increased section spacing.

Desktop:
- Maximum content width: 1280px.
- 4 product columns.
- 2-column product details.
- Horizontal navigation.
- Larger editorial sections.

Breakpoints:
Mobile:     < 640px
Tablet:     640px–1023px
Desktop:    >= 1024px

06. COMPONENTS
--------------------------------------------------
HEADER
- White background.
- 64px height.
- Thin bottom border.
- Sticky positioning.
- Logo aligned left.
- Navigation centered.
- Shopping bag aligned right.

BUTTONS
Primary:

- Fully rounded.
- 44–52px height.

- Black text.

- Rounded corners: 8px.
- Object-fit: cover.
- Product title: 12px.

- Dark overlay.
- White bottom-aligned title.

INPUT
- White background.
- Neutral border.
- Rounded full or 8px.
- Minimum touch height: 44px.

PRODUCT DETAIL
- Large product gallery.
- Thumbnail navigation.
- Clear product title and price.
- Color selector.
